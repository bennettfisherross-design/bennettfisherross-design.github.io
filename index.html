// About

I'm a mechanical engineering student at the University of Wisconsin–Madison pursuing a focus on robotics and machine learning. I like taking a project from a rough sketch through CAD, manufacturing, programming, and a working — if occasionally smoking — prototype.

Almost everything on this site is only possible because of the support of my robotics teams: 77717 Robotics (a Vex Robotics team I founded in 2020), and Wisconsin Robotics (Outreach team)
