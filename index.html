// About

I'm a mechanical engineering student at the University of Wisconsin–Madison with a focus on mechanism design, rapid prototyping, and robotics. I like taking a project from a rough sketch through CAD, manufacturing, and a working — if occasionally smoking — prototype.

Most of what's on this site started as a "what if I built..." question. Each project page walks through the design process, the parts that failed, and the iteration that followed.
